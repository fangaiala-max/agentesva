  - paragraph: Prioritize recurring work with clear rules, accessible data, and a measurable outcome.

    - heading "The practical takeaway" [level=2]
    - paragraph: "Record repetitive tasks for a week: frequency, time per case, systems involved, waiting time, and common errors. Score candidates on volume, effort, impact, integration difficulty, and risk. Good first projects have an owner, representative examples, and a result that can be checked. Avoid starting with poorly understood, exceptional, or high-impact work. Select one bounded process and establish a baseline before building so the later result can be assessed against something concrete."
    - heading "Keep these points in mind." [level=2]
    - list:
      - listitem: Standardize the process before moving it into software.
      - listitem: Favor meaningful impact and manageable complexity for the first pilot.
      - listitem: Keep human review where ambiguity or consequences matter.
